# CFX Authentication

Some JX Scripts resources use **Cfx.re (FiveM) asset escrow** for distribution and licensing. This page explains how authentication works and how to manage your assets.

## What is Cfx.re Asset Escrow?

Cfx.re asset escrow is the official FiveM system for distributing paid resources. When you purchase a script through the [Cfx.re Tebex store](https://fivem.net), the resource is tied to your Cfx.re account and can only be downloaded through your Keymaster.

## Accessing Your Resources

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log into Keymaster">
    Go to [keymaster.fivem.net](https://keymaster.fivem.net) and sign in with your Cfx.re account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Your Assets">
    Navigate to **Granted Assets** to see all resources you've purchased or been granted access to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the Resource">
    Click on the resource you want to download. You'll receive the latest version of the script.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer to Server">
    Place the downloaded resource folder into your server's `resources` directory and add `ensure resource_name` to your `server.cfg`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Server Licensing

<Tip>
  Each Cfx.re server requires its own **server license key** to run. Make sure your server key is properly configured in your `server.cfg` file.
</Tip>

Your purchased assets are tied to your Cfx.re account, **not** a specific server. You can use them on any server that is registered under your account.

## Common Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Asset not showing in Keymaster">
    Make sure you're logged into the same Cfx.re account you used to make the purchase. If you used a different account, you'll need to contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Resource won't start on server">
    Ensure your server's license key is valid and associated with your Cfx.re account. Expired or invalid keys will prevent escrowed resources from loading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transfer to another account">
    Cfx.re does not officially support transferring assets between accounts. Purchases are final and bound to the purchasing account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
