# How To Use

Learn how to create and manage PvP zones as an admin, and how to use redzone features as a player.

---

## For Admins — Creating Zones

Zones are created entirely in-game using the visual zone creator. No config editing required.

### Opening the Creator

You must have admin permissions (configured in `Config.Creator.permissions`).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Keybind" icon="keyboard">
    Press **F7** to open the zone creator (remappable in FiveM keybind settings).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Command" icon="terminal">
    Type `/rzcreator` in chat to open the zone creator.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Creating a New Zone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Creator">
    Press F7 or type `/rzcreator`. The creator UI opens and enters freecam mode.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Position the Zone">
    Move the freecam to the location where you want to place the zone. The zone center will be placed at the freecam position.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Zone Settings">
    In the creator panel, set the following:
    
    - **Name** — A display name for the zone
    - **Radius** — How large the zone is (default: 40 units)
    - **Color** — The zone's color on the map and in the UI
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Entry Rewards">
    Configure which weapon players receive when entering the zone:
    
    - Select from available weapon types
    - The weapon is given automatically on entry and removed on exit
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Kill Rewards">
    Set money rewards for different actions:
    
    - **Kill Reward** — Money earned per kill
    - **Headshot Reward** — Bonus money for headshot kills
    - **Streak Rewards** — Extra money at kill streak thresholds (e.g., 3, 5, 10 kills)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Zone Rules">
    Toggle zone rules:
    
    - **Delete Vehicles** — Remove vehicles that enter the zone
    - **Use Routing Bucket** — Isolate the zone in its own routing bucket
    - **Block Cross-Zone Damage** — Prevent players in different zones from damaging each other
    - **Enable Marker** — Show a ground marker at the zone border
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the Zone">
    Click save. The zone is stored in `data/zones.json` and becomes active immediately — no restart needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Managing Existing Zones

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Editing a Zone" icon="pen-to-square">
    1. Open the creator (F7 or `/rzcreator`)
    2. Select the zone you want to edit from the list
    3. Modify any settings (radius, rewards, rules, etc.)
    4. Save changes — they apply immediately
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Deleting a Zone" icon="trash">
    1. Open the creator (F7 or `/rzcreator`)
    2. Select the zone to delete
    3. Click the delete button
    4. The zone is removed from the map and from `data/zones.json`
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Zones are saved to `data/zones.json`. You can also back up this file manually if you want to preserve your zone configurations.
</Tip>

## For Players — Using Redzones

### Entering a Zone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a Redzone">
    Active redzones appear on the map as blips with radius circles (if blips are enabled).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Walk Into the Zone">
    Enter the zone boundary. When you enter:
    
    - You receive the entry weapon automatically (if configured)
    - Your HUD may change (depends on server setup)
    - You enter the zone's routing bucket (if enabled) — only other players in the same zone are visible
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fight Other Players">
    Engage in PvP combat within the zone. Rewards are earned based on your performance:
    
    - **Kill** — Earn money for each player you eliminate
    - **Headshot** — Earn bonus money for headshot kills
    - **Kill Streak** — Reach streak thresholds (e.g., 3, 5, 10 kills) for extra rewards
  </Step>

  <Step title="Death and Revive">
    When you die in a zone, you're automatically revived after a short delay (default: 800ms). No need to call EMS or wait for respawn — you're back in the fight quickly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave the Zone">
    Walk out of the zone boundary to exit PvP mode. Your HUD is restored and entry weapons are removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Player Commands

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="/rzcolor — Color Picker" icon="palette">
    Customize your personal zone color overlay.

    1. Type `/rzcolor` in chat
    2. A color picker UI opens
    3. Choose from preset colors or pick a custom color
    4. Your selected color changes how the zone visuals appear for you

    <Info>
      This is a personal preference — other players won't see your color choice.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="/selectstats — Stats Layout" icon="chart-bar">
    Change how your kill statistics are displayed.

    1. Type `/selectstats` in chat
    2. Choose from three layout options:
       - **Hex** (`stat1`) — Hexagonal display
       - **Table** (`stat2`) — Table/grid display
       - **Rail** (`stat3`) — Rail/bar display
    3. Your preference is saved for future sessions
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="/rzlb — Leaderboard" icon="trophy">
    View the kill leaderboard across all zones.

    1. Type `/rzlb` in chat
    2. The leaderboard UI opens showing top players
    3. See kills, headshots, streaks, and ranking
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reward System

### How Rewards Work

| Action | Default Reward | Description |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Kill | $500 | Money earned for each player kill |
| Headshot | $250 | Bonus on top of kill reward |
| 3 Kill Streak | $1,000 | Bonus at 3 consecutive kills |
| 5 Kill Streak | $2,500 | Bonus at 5 consecutive kills |
| 10 Kill Streak | $5,000 | Bonus at 10 consecutive kills |

<Note>
  All reward values are configurable per-zone in the creator. The values above are the defaults from `Config.ZoneDefaults`.
</Note>

### Reward Modes

- **Random** — When multiple reward entries exist, one is chosen randomly
- **All** — All configured rewards are given at once

Kill and headshot rewards default to `'random'` mode. Streak rewards default to `'all'` mode.

## Zone Mechanics

### Routing Buckets

When `useRoutingBucket` is enabled for a zone, players inside that zone are moved to a separate routing bucket. This means:

- Only players in the same zone can see each other
- Players outside the zone cannot interact with or see zone players
- Each zone gets its own bucket, starting from `Config.RoutingBuckets.base`

### Cross-Zone Damage Blocking

When `blockCrossZoneDamage` is enabled, players in different zones cannot damage each other — even if the zones are physically close together.

### Vehicle Deletion

When `deleteVehicles` is enabled, any vehicle that enters the zone boundary is automatically deleted. The cleanup interval is controlled by `Config.VehicleDelete.interval`.

## Tips

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Zone Placement" icon="map-pin">
    Place zones in open areas away from spawn points. Avoid putting zones near mission areas or garages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reward Balance" icon="scale-balanced">
    Start with lower rewards and increase based on player activity. Too-high rewards can destabilize your economy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Routing Buckets" icon="layer-group">
    Enable routing buckets for the best experience. It prevents outside interference and makes zones feel more contained.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zone Size" icon="arrows-left-right">
    A radius of 30-50 is good for close-quarters combat. Use 80-120 for larger areas with vehicles or sniping.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
