# How To Use

Learn the full rental workflow — from finding a rental point to driving away in your new vehicle.

---

## Renting a Vehicle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a Rental Point">
    Rental locations appear on the map as blips (if enabled in the config). Head to the nearest rental point.

    <Info>
      If blips are disabled, ask your server admin for rental locations.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Interact with the Rental Point">
    Depending on your server's configuration:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Target System" icon="bullseye">
        If the server uses **ox_target** or **qb-target**, aim at the rental ped and click the interaction option.
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Marker / TextUI" icon="display">
        If no target system is configured, walk up to the rental ped or marker. A TextUI prompt appears when you're close enough — press the indicated key to interact.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse Vehicle Categories">
    The rental UI opens showing vehicle categories:

    - **Compact** — Small, affordable city cars
    - **Sedan** — Mid-range sedans
    - **Sports** — Performance vehicles
    - **SUV** — Large utility vehicles
    - **Super** — High-end supercars

    Click a category to see available vehicles with their names and prices.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select and Preview a Vehicle">
    Click on a vehicle to select it. A preview of the vehicle spawns at the rental point so you can see exactly what you're renting before committing.

    <Tip>
      Browse multiple vehicles to compare — a new preview spawns each time you select a different vehicle.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize the Color (Optional)">
    If color customization is enabled, you can pick a color for your vehicle before renting:

    - Choose from the preset color palette
    - The preview vehicle updates to show your selected color
    - Each color has a label (e.g., White, Black, Red, Blue)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Price">
    The UI shows the rental price based on your server's pricing mode:

    - **One-Time** — Pay once and keep the vehicle
    - **Hourly** — Pay per hour of rental time
    - If your server allows mode selection, choose between the two

    Payment is deducted from cash or bank depending on server config.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the Rental">
    Click the confirm button to finalize the rental:

    - Money is deducted from your account
    - The vehicle spawns at the designated spawn point
    - Vehicle keys are given automatically
    - The rental is now active
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drive Away">
    Walk to the spawn point, get in your vehicle, and go! The vehicle is yours until it's returned, despawned, or the rental expires (if using hourly mode).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Active Rentals

### Rental Limits

Each player can have a maximum number of active rentals at the same time (default: 4). If you've reached the limit, you'll need to return a rental before renting another vehicle.

### What Counts as Active

A rental is considered active as long as the vehicle exists in the game world. The rental ends when:

- The vehicle is despawned or destroyed
- The server restarts
- The hourly rental time expires (if using hourly mode)

---

## Payment

### Payment Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Deducted from your cash/pocket money |
| **Bank** | Deducted from your bank account |

The payment method is configured by your server admin. Some servers may use ox_inventory money items for cash payments.

### Pricing Modes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="One-Time" icon="money-bill">
    Pay the full price once. The vehicle is yours until it's gone.

    **Example**: A Blista costs $500 — you pay $500 and drive it as long as you want.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Hourly" icon="clock">
    Pay per hour of rental time. The price shown in the UI is the hourly rate.

    **Example**: A Blista costs $500/hour — renting for 3 hours costs $1,500.

    The minimum rental time is configured by the server (default: 1 hour).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Tips

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Preview Before You Pay" icon="eye">
    Always preview the vehicle first. The preview spawns at the rental point so you can see the model and your chosen color before spending money.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check Your Balance" icon="wallet">
    Make sure you have enough cash or bank balance before trying to rent. The UI shows the price, but the transaction will fail if you're short.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watch Your Rental Count" icon="hashtag">
    You can only have a limited number of active rentals. If you can't rent a new vehicle, check how many you already have.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Park Safely" icon="square-parking">
    Rental vehicles aren't saved to your garage. If it despawns or gets destroyed, the rental is over. Park in safe areas.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
