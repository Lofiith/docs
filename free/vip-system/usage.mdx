# How To Use

Learn how to access and use VIP features in-game, from opening the menu to using each perk.

---

## Getting VIP Access

Before you can use VIP features, you need a VIP role in the server's Discord:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord Server">
    Join the server's Discord community if you haven't already
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a VIP Role">
    Obtain a VIP or VIP+ role — this is typically through a purchase, giveaway, or community reward (managed by server staff)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to FiveM">
    Join the FiveM server. Your Discord roles are checked automatically on connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Sync">
    Allow a few seconds after joining for Discord data to load. Your VIP status is then available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you just received a VIP role, you may need to reconnect to the FiveM server or use `/viprefresh` for it to take effect.
</Note>

---

## Opening the VIP Menu

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Keybind" icon="keyboard">
    Press **F5** to open the VIP menu (default keybind, remappable in FiveM settings).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Command" icon="terminal">
    Type `/vipmenu` in chat to open the VIP menu.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The menu displays all available features based on your VIP tier, along with prices and cooldown status.

## Using Features

### VIP Features (Both Tiers)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Full Heal — $500" icon="heart">
    Instantly restore your health to 100%.

    - **Price**: $500 (default)
    - **Cooldown**: 5 minutes
    - **How**: Open VIP menu → Select "Full Heal"
    - Money is deducted automatically
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Full Armor — $750" icon="shield-halved">
    Instantly restore your armor to 100%.

    - **Price**: $750 (default)
    - **Cooldown**: 5 minutes
    - **How**: Open VIP menu → Select "Full Armor"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vehicle Repair — $1,000" icon="wrench">
    Fully repair your current vehicle (engine, body, and visual damage).

    - **Price**: $1,000 (default)
    - **Cooldown**: 10 minutes
    - **Requirement**: Must be in a vehicle
    - **How**: Get in a vehicle → Open VIP menu → Select "Repair Vehicle"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vehicle Wash — $200" icon="droplet">
    Clean your current vehicle, removing dirt and grime.

    - **Price**: $200 (default)
    - **Cooldown**: 2 minutes
    - **Requirement**: Must be in a vehicle
    - **How**: Get in a vehicle → Open VIP menu → Select "Wash Vehicle"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clothing Menu — Free" icon="shirt">
    Open your server's skin/clothing customization menu.

    - **Price**: Free
    - **Cooldown**: 1 minute
    - **Requirement**: A supported skin menu must be installed on the server
    - **How**: Open VIP menu → Select "Clothing Menu"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### VIP+ Exclusive Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="God Mode — $2,000" icon="shield">
    Become invincible for 5 minutes. No damage can hurt you.

    - **Price**: $2,000 (default)
    - **Duration**: 5 minutes
    - **Cooldown**: 30 minutes
    - **Tier**: VIP+ only
    - **How**: Open VIP menu → Select "God Mode"
    - The effect automatically ends after the duration expires

    <Warning>
      God mode deactivates automatically when the timer runs out or when the resource restarts.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invisibility — $1,000" icon="eye-slash">
    Become invisible to all other players for 3 minutes.

    - **Price**: $1,000 (default)
    - **Duration**: 3 minutes
    - **Cooldown**: 20 minutes
    - **Tier**: VIP+ only
    - **How**: Open VIP menu → Select "Invisibility"
    - Other players cannot see you during the effect

    <Note>
      You can still interact with the world and take damage while invisible (unless god mode is also active).
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## VIP Shop

The VIP Shop is an exclusive item store with tiered pricing. VIP+ members get lower prices and access to exclusive items.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open VIP Menu">
    Press F5 or type `/vipmenu`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select VIP Shop">
    Choose the "VIP Shop" option from the menu
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Items">
    See available items with names, prices, and quantities
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase">
    Select an item to buy. Money is deducted and items are added to your inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Tier Differences

| Aspect | VIP | VIP+ |
|--------|-----|------|
| Item prices | Standard | Discounted |
| Item quantities | Standard | Higher |
| Exclusive items | ❌ | ✅ (lockpick, repair kit, etc.) |

## Quick Travel

VIP+ members can teleport to preset locations around the map.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open VIP Menu">
    Press F5 or type `/vipmenu`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Quick Travel">
    Choose "Quick Travel" from the menu
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Destination">
    Browse available locations with their prices
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select a location. Money is deducted and you're teleported instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Default Locations**:

| Location | Price |
|----------|-------|
| Hospital | $200 |
| City Hall | $200 |
| Airport | $300 |
| Beach | $250 |

<Note>
  Quick Travel is VIP+ only by default. Locations and prices are configured by the server admin.
</Note>

## Useful Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/vipmenu` | Open the VIP menu (alternative to F5) |
| `/vipstatus` | Check your current VIP tier and status |
| `/viprefresh` | Force refresh your Discord role cache — use this if you just got a new role |

## Cooldowns

Most features have cooldown timers to prevent spam. When a feature is on cooldown:

- The menu shows the remaining cooldown time
- You cannot use the feature until the timer expires
- Each feature has its own independent cooldown

**Default Cooldowns**:

| Feature | Cooldown |
|---------|----------|
| Heal | 5 minutes |
| Armor | 5 minutes |
| Vehicle Repair | 10 minutes |
| Vehicle Wash | 2 minutes |
| Clothing Menu | 1 minute |
| God Mode | 30 minutes |
| Invisibility | 20 minutes |
